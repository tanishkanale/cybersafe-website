Know the Threats

Understand common cyber threats and learn how to protect yourself online.

🎣

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to steal your personal information.

Stay protected:

Verify senders and avoid suspicious links.

🔐

Password Theft

Attackers may try to obtain your passwords and access your accounts.

Stay protected:

Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or compromise information.

Stay protected:

Download apps only from trusted sources.

📱

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, giveaway messages and impersonation attempts.

Stay protected:

Verify profiles and never share OTPs.

🌐

Unsafe Websites

Fake or untrustworthy websites may attempt to collect your information.

Stay protected:

Check website addresses before entering personal details.

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for every account. Avoid easily guessed information.

🔑

📩

Avoid Suspicious Links

📱

Safe Public Wi-Fi

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common cybersecurity questions.

1. What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting devices, networks and personal information from digital attacks.

2. What is phishing?

Phishing is a scam where attackers use fake messages or websites to steal personal information.

3. How can I create a strong password?

Use a long, unique password with a combination of words, numbers and symbols. Avoid reusing passwords.

4. What is two-factor authentication?

It adds another verification step when logging into an account, making it harder for others to access it.

5. What should I do if I face online fraud?

Contact 1930 immediately for financial cyber fraud and report it at the official cybercrime portal.

6. Is public Wi-Fi safe?

Public Wi-Fi can be risky. Avoid sensitive transactions on unsecured networks.
