What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity means protecting computers, mobile phones, networks and personal information from digital threats. It helps us stay safe while using the internet.

Types of Cyber Crimes

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails and messages designed to steal personal information.

🔐 Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without permission.

💳 Online Fraud

Scams that trick people into sending money or sharing banking details.

🦠 Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices or compromise information.

👤 Cyberbullying

Harassing or threatening someone through digital platforms.

📱 Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, suspicious links and misleading online offers.

Online Safety Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Use secure websites (HTTPS).
  • Think before sharing personal information.
  • Verify unknown callers and messages.
