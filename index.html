Essential Safety Tips

✅ Use a different strong password for every account.

✅ Enable two-factor authentication.

✅ Never share OTPs or passwords.

✅ Avoid clicking unknown links.

✅ Keep your apps and devices updated.

✅ Report suspicious online activity.

STAY ALERT • STAY SAFE

Know the Threats

Understand common cyber threats and learn how to protect yourself online.

🎣

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to steal your personal information.

Stay protected:

Verify senders and avoid suspicious links.

🔐

Password Theft

Attackers may try to obtain your passwords and access your accounts.

Stay protected:

Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or compromise information.

Stay protected:

Download apps only from trusted sources.

📱

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, giveaway messages and impersonation attempts.

Stay protected:

Verify profiles and never share OTPs.

🌐

Unsafe Websites

Fake or untrustworthy websites may attempt to collect your information.

Stay protected:

Check website addresses before entering personal details.
